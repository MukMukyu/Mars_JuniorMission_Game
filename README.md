## About the game
My Little Colonist lets you live the experience of living in a small outpost in the Gale Crater, next to the Curiosity rover.

Here you'll have to manage your little resources to survive the sols and the harsh weather that Mars have.

- Drink water
- Eat well
- Clean your solar pannels
- Fix them
- And don't run out of energy!...
